Skybee — a free open-source Bluesky client

Skybee is a free, open-source app for the Bluesky social network (AT Protocol). It runs in the browser and as an app for Windows, Linux, Android and iPhone. Sign in with your Bluesky account, ideally with an app password.

Open SkybeeDownload the apps

What Skybee can do

  • Multi-column deck: several feeds, lists and searches side by side, even as different accounts
  • Custom feeds without a server: merge sources, keywords and filters, all on your device
  • Chats, group chats, reactions and replies; notifications, including browser notifications with the tab closed
  • Post scheduler, multiple drafts, bookmarks with folders and search
  • Profile analytics: follower growth, engagement, who unfollowed, who does not follow back
  • Skybee-only profile looks: colours, a status, an Apple Music track
  • Skybee, Liquid Glass (iOS-style) and Material Design 3 themes, accent colours, fonts
  • Many interface languages, including English, Ukrainian, Polish, German, French and Spanish
  • Moderation: content filters, muted words, third-party labelers, lists

Where it runs

  • Web: skybee.online — nothing to install, can be added to the home screen
  • Windows: installer with auto-update
  • Linux: Flatpak
  • Android: APK
  • iPhone: IPA to install with Sideloadly or AltStore

FAQ

What is Skybee?

Skybee is an unofficial open-source Bluesky client: a different interface to the same network and the same accounts.

Is it free?

Yes. Skybee is free and its code is on GitHub.

Is it safe to sign in?

Use a Bluesky app password: you can revoke it at any time. Skybee uses your main password only if you choose that.

Is there a Skybee app for Windows and phones?

Yes: a Windows app, a Linux Flatpak, an Android APK and an iPhone file. All downloads are under Releases on GitHub.
